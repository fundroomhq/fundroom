# @fundroom/avscan-clamd

## 1.0.0-rc.1

### Patch Changes

- [#31](https://github.com/fundroomhq/fundroom/pull/31) [`8721cd2`](https://github.com/fundroomhq/fundroom/commit/8721cd23dcf46f3e9b4de7317f4e0c1feab56275) Thanks [@fundroomio](https://github.com/fundroomio)! - Scan failures that retrying cannot fix are now final instead of retried until the dead letters. A data-room file the virus scanner cannot scan in full (over its size limit, or the engine stopped at one of its limits) is marked `error` with the reason, `document.ingested` is published with `scanStatus: "error"`, and the document reads "failed"; uploading the same file again retries the scan instead of answering 409, while the same bytes as an infected file get a 409 "the virus scanner flagged this file". An e-signature artifact that cannot be scanned in full fails collection with `artifact_unscannable`. A multipart upload over the part limit is refused with 413 before anything is created, and every scan names its caller (`data-room`, `esign`, `round`) for the scanner metrics. A scanner outage no longer shows "Scan failed" while the ingest job is still retrying: the file stays Processing (`scanning`, with the reason in its scan detail) and only the last attempt records `error` before the job is dead-lettered; a retried dead letter scans again. For that, a job handler now sees which delivery it is (`ActiveJob.attempt: { count, limit }`, from pg-boss's retry count and limit). One ingest job scans a blob at a time (a conditional claim) and the promotion into the content-addressed key holds the blob row, so a re-upload or a second job can no longer race a scan or overwrite a promoted object; re-uploading a file whose scan failed also re-ingests the versions that failed with it. A stopping or expired job aborts its scan (`ScanInput.signal`), the scan deadline is capped at 20 minutes (`CLAMD_MAX_DEADLINE_MS`, now defined once in `@fundroom/ports`), the e-sign collect job expires after 30 minutes, and `doctor` warns when `CLAMD_TIMEOUT_MS` alone passes that cap or when `ESIGN_MAX_ARTIFACT_BYTES` exceeds `AV_MAX_SCAN_BYTES`. Migration `data-room/0009` adds a nullable `scan_claimed_by` column (no rewrite).

- [#31](https://github.com/fundroomhq/fundroom/pull/31) [`8721cd2`](https://github.com/fundroomhq/fundroom/commit/8721cd23dcf46f3e9b4de7317f4e0c1feab56275) Thanks [@fundroomio](https://github.com/fundroomio)! - **Security:** @fundroom/server no longer crashes when clamd drops a connection mid-scan: every clamd exchange settles once, closes its socket and releases the upload stream, and an unhandled promise rejection is logged (`process.unhandled_rejection`) and counted instead of exiting the process. Scan errors now carry a reason: `too_large` and `partial` (clamd `Heuristics.Limits.Exceeded`, "could not be scanned in full" — no longer reported as infected) are final, `unavailable` and `timeout` are retried. New keys `AV_MAX_SCAN_BYTES` (clamd's `StreamMaxLength`; also caps the upload ceiling), `CLAMD_CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS` and `CLAMD_MIN_BYTES_PER_SECOND`; new metrics `fundroom.avscan.scans`, `.duration`, `.errors`, `.up` and `.signature_age_seconds`; `GET /platform/health` reports the scanner's last probe.

## 1.0.0-rc.0

### Minor Changes

- [`538f8b3`](https://github.com/fundroomhq/fundroom/commit/538f8b3b40aafd77196e2c0d6ba66bb8ebe3ad5b) Thanks [@fundroomio](https://github.com/fundroomio)! - Data room. New module package `@fundroom/module-data-room` (`dataroom` schema: folders with `ltree` paths and index numbering, content-addressed blobs with the scan state machine, documents with immutable versions, renditions, extracted page text, staged uploads; `/api/v1/data-room/*`: tree, folders, templates (Seed, Series A DD, Board), documents, versions, protection, legal hold, recycle bin, purge, uploads, settings; delivery routes for thumbnails, watermarked page images, in-document search, watermarked/original downloads; jobs `data-room.ingest`, `data-room.purge`, `data-room.reconcile`; the `document_list` content hydrator; tus raw route for the filesystem driver). New ports `VirusScanPort` and `DocumentRenderPort` with adapters `@fundroom/avscan-noop`, `@fundroom/avscan-clamd` (INSTREAM) and `@fundroom/render-pdfium` (PDFium WASM rasteriser, pdf-lib sanitise/watermark, sharp). `@fundroom/config`: `AV_DRIVER`, `CLAMD_HOST/PORT/TIMEOUT_MS`, `RENDER_MAX_BYTES`. `@fundroom/module-kit`: `ModuleServices` gains `crypto`, `scanner`, `renderer`, `limits`, `queue.sendInTransaction`, `authz.bump`; manifests may declare `jobs` as a factory over services and `rawRoutes` mounted outside the JSON body limit. `@fundroom/authz` + `@fundroom/db` migration `0005_access_path_inheritance`: a rule on an ancestor path covers every node below it regardless of kind (folder grants reach documents), `GrantRepo`/`PolicyRepo.rewritePaths` for folder moves. `@fundroom/domain`: `dataRoom` workspace settings, `document.ingested` event. `@fundroom/web`: `/admin/data-room` (browser, uploads, document detail, recycle bin, settings) and the investor `/data-room` browser + secure viewer.
