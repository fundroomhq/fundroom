# Privacy notice for the {{company.name}} investor portal

## In plain English

This portal is private. To let you in and to keep the material in it safe, we have to know who you are and
keep a record of what you opened. So: we record your name and email, the fact that you signed in, and which
documents and updates you viewed or downloaded, and when. That record exists because we are handling
confidential company information and because, if there is ever a question about who saw what, we need to be
able to answer it.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertising networks. We do not build a profile of you for
anyone other than {{company.name}}, and we do not use it to make an automated decision about you.

Beyond the access record, we may also measure engagement — how long a page was open, which sections of a
document were read, whether an emailed update was opened. Where we do that, you can see it below, and in the
UK and the EU we will not do it unless you have agreed, or we will let you turn it off.

## What we collect

**Information you or your introducer give us**

- Identity and contact details: name, email address, and where you provide them, telephone number,
  organisation, job title, and postal address.
- Investor context: the group you belong to in the portal, your country and (in the US) state of residence,
  how we came to know you and when that relationship began.
- Accreditation, sophistication or qualified-investor answers, and any supporting evidence you or a
  third-party verifier provide. This can include financial information. We keep the minimum we can: where a
  verification service can confirm your status without us holding your documents, we prefer that route, and
  documents you do upload are encrypted and deleted on the schedule below.
- Anything you type into the portal: replies to updates, questions, and requests for access.

**Information generated by your use of the portal**

- Account and security records: sign-in events, one-time codes, sessions and devices, and failed attempts.
- Portal access logs: the IP address, the browser and device type, the time, and the page or API route.
- Document view and download records: which document, which version, which pages, at what time, and which
  version of this notice and of any confidentiality agreement you had accepted at that moment.
- Engagement analytics, where enabled: time a page or document section was open, scroll and page progress,
  and whether an emailed update was opened or its links clicked.
- Email delivery records: that a message was sent to you, and whether it was delivered, bounced, or reported
  as spam.

We do not knowingly collect data from children, and the portal is not intended for them.

## Cookies, tracking and Global Privacy Control

The portal uses a strictly necessary session cookie so that you stay signed in. Any analytics beyond that is
governed by the cookie notice, and by the consent rules described above.

We honour the **Global Privacy Control** signal. If your browser sends it, we treat it as an opt-out of
engagement analytics and of any sharing that would count as a sale or as sharing for advertising, without you
having to do anything else. You may also opt out at any time in the portal, or by emailing us.

Where the portal is embedded in another website, that website's consent manager controls tracking on the page
around it, and the portal defers to it.
