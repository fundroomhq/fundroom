## What we do

- Keyboard access to every function, with a visible focus indicator and skip links.
- Semantic structure, form labels, and status messages announced to assistive technology.
- Text contrast meeting the AA thresholds in both light and dark themes.
- Respect for reduced-motion and increased-contrast preferences.
- Charts accompanied by an equivalent data table.
- Documents rendered with a real text layer where the source file has one.
