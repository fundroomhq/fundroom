**Entities**

- [ ] **Assets test.** The entity has total assets over **US$5,000,000** and was not formed for the specific
      purpose of acquiring the securities offered. (Applies to corporations, partnerships, LLCs, business

- [ ] **All-accredited entity.** Every equity owner of the entity is an accredited investor.
- [ ] **Institutional.** The entity is a bank, savings and loan, registered broker-dealer, insurance company,
      registered investment company, business development company, small business investment company, rural
      business investment company, or a registered investment adviser or exempt reporting adviser.
- [ ] **Plan.** The entity is an employee benefit plan whose investment decisions are made by a plan
