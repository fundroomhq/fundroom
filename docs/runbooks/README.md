# Runbooks

One file per operational procedure, written for whoever runs the install rather than for whoever wrote it: what to check, in what order, and what each failure means. Steps that need a decision say who makes it.

- [accreditation.md](accreditation.md) — accreditation vendors (VerifyInvestor, Parallel Markets): the `round.verification_*` jobs, verifications stuck `pending`, failed vendor starts, refused callbacks, a connection in `error`, re-running the status sync, and reading the vendor decisions from the audit log
- [ai-assist.md](ai-assist.md) — AI assist: choosing a model provider (Ollama and other self-hosted OpenAI-compatible servers, a hosted OpenAI-compatible API, Anthropic), Ollama's context length, self-hosted versus third-party hosting and what leaves the host, workspace opt-in and the provider acknowledgement, budgets and limits, changing or turning off the provider, and every error code
- [acme-failures.md](acme-failures.md) — certificates that will not issue or renew for the install's own hostname: reading Caddy's ACME error, CAA, rate limits, the ZeroSSL fallback and `ACME_CA`, and why multi-tenant slug subdomains get no certificate from the shipped edge
- [break-glass.md](break-glass.md) — host-operator access to a workspace's data past row-level security: when it is justified, ticket discipline, `fundroom break-glass open|sql|close|list`, what the workspace's owners are told and see in their audit log, and what the control does not protect against
- [audit-anchoring.md](audit-anchoring.md) — external audit anchoring: choosing RFC 3161 TSAs and/or Sigstore Rekor, pinning TSA certificates and the Rekor log key and origin (with the commands to fetch them), RFC 3161 time versus Rekor presence, what leaves the host (one 32-byte root a day), checking anchors, verifying a proof as a third party with the CLI and with OpenSSL, Rekor shard rotation, and failure codes
- [av-failure.md](av-failure.md) — the virus scanner: what `noop` and `clamd` do to an upload, `clamd` down (fail-closed, retries, dead letters), the stream-size limit, signature updates that fail silently, and infected files
- [backup-and-restore.md](backup-and-restore.md) — the three parts of a backup (database, file storage, master key); Tier 0 `pg_dump`, Tier 1 pgBackRest with point-in-time recovery, Tier 2 CloudNativePG; health checks, the monthly restore drill, in-place PITR, restoring onto a new host, and what becomes unrecoverable without the key
- [billing.md](billing.md) — managed-host billing: Stripe setup (keys, the plan's price, the `fundroom_staff_seats` / `fundroom_storage_gb` meters and the `BILLING_METER_*_EVENT` names, the webhook endpoint, its API version and event list, the Customer Portal), test mode, the manual driver, grace and billing suspension, and webhooks that fail or do not arrive
- [central-auth.md](central-auth.md) — `CENTRAL_AUTH=on`: signing in once on the canonical host and handing a workspace-bound session to custom domains and slug hosts, the `?error=` codes a user can land on, step-up and sign-out on a bound session, passkeys on custom domains, and the residual risk of tenant-controlled DNS
- [control-plane.md](control-plane.md) — the managed-host control plane (`CONTROL_PLANE=on`): granting operators and opening `/platform`, why the console says not found, creating, suspending and unsuspending workspaces (holds, incl. `relocation`), cells and `421 wrong_cell`, plans and `402 plan_limit`, daily usage, and the audit actions
- [csp-reports.md](csp-reports.md) — CSP violation reports: what the collector keeps and drops, reading the `fundroom_csp_violations_total` metric, the Trusted Types report-only stance and the path to enforcement, and configuring `security.txt` (with the `BASE_PATH` caveat)
- [custom-domains.md](custom-domains.md) — add, repair or move a workspace's custom domain; certificate issuance failures (CAA, ACME rate limits, the `ask` endpoint); Cloudflare for SaaS (the API token, fallback origin, CNAME target, provider-gated activation, and `CLOUDFLARE_TRUSTED_PROXY`)
- [document-rendering.md](document-rendering.md) — page renditions, watermarks and emailed chart images: what the `render` readiness check means, what a blank watermark is telling you, and how to tell a renderer fault from a bad document
- [embed-troubleshooting.md](embed-troubleshooting.md) — diagnose an embed that will not render: `frame-ancestors`, blocked partitioned cookies, host CSP and ad blockers, double-iframing builders, and what `embed.origin_rejected` means
- [esign.md](esign.md) — e-signature envelopes: status sync and the `esign.*` jobs, envelopes stuck in `sent`, refused vendor callbacks (and Dropbox Sign clearing its callback URL), vendor outages, signed copies not collected (`artifact_*`), orphaned drafts, and rotating callback secrets and vendor credentials
- [forensic-watermarking.md](forensic-watermarking.md) — invisible per-viewer page marks: what is marked and what is not, turning it on, tracing a leaked screenshot or photo, reading z scores and verdicts without accusing the wrong person, errors, what is stored, keys and rotation, and the share-link `forceWatermark` fix
- [incident-response.md](incident-response.md) — sizing an incident, the first fifteen minutes, preserving evidence (`audit verify`, `audit checkpoint`, signed audit exports), containment, and who must be told by when (the GDPR 72-hour clock, workspace owners, investors)
- [install-and-upgrade.md](install-and-upgrade.md) — choosing Compose, Coolify, Helm or a PaaS template; where migrations run on each; the first-run setup token per platform; verifying image and chart signatures; upgrading; what a rollback can mean when migrations are forward-only; the update notice and how to opt out
- [openfga.md](openfga.md) — the optional OpenFGA engine: what it sees and what stays in Postgres, running it, the shadow-to-enforce rollout, reading the mismatch metric, sync state, fail-closed behaviour and sizing
- [path-mount.md](path-mount.md) — the portal under a path of another site (`PATH_MOUNTS`): everything 404s, assets 404 or a blank page, sign-in loops (cookie name and `Path`), CSRF 403s from a request that is not mounted, the edge's 503 (`FUNDROOM_BASE_PATH` and the health check), cached private pages, shared rate-limit budgets and failing uploads
- [queue-backlog.md](queue-backlog.md) — pg-boss queues, the outbox and dead letters: finding where a backlog is, what `WORKER_CONCURRENCY` does and does not scale, adding worker capacity, and `fundroom jobs dlq list|retry|discard`
- [releasing.md](releasing.md) — for maintainers: how a merged "version packages" PR becomes the `v<version>` git tag, the draft-then-published GitHub release and the image (scanned and signed before any tag names it); release candidates, `latest` and the other moving tags; changesets that must bump the core; resuming a failed release and why `X.Y.Z` never moves; and why the workflow names must not change
- [residency.md](residency.md) — data residency: declaring the region an install's data lives in (`DATA_REGION`, `BACKUP_LOCATION`) and what tenants see, what is and is not pinned to it (email, telemetry, vendors, the edge), standing up a cell in a second region, the shared directory database and what happens when it is down, moving a workspace between regions (downtime, what is carried and lost, failure codes), and checking it all
- [rotate-keys.md](rotate-keys.md) — the master key ring (`SECRET_KEY_RING`), what a rotation re-wraps and what it leaves on the old key, what signs people out, and the standalone secrets (metrics token, ESP webhooks, OIDC, database, storage, mail)
- [sanctions.md](sanctions.md) — sanctions screening of tenant companies: the `ofac` and `opensanctions` drivers, what the OFAC download needs (User-Agent, the S3 redirect, egress), the OpenSanctions licence, working the review queue, what a potential match is, re-screening, errors and retention
- [sso.md](sso.md) — staff SSO and SCIM: reading `sso_error` codes, configurations that will not save (OIDC discovery, SAML metadata), rotating the IdP signing certificate and the OIDC client secret, a company locked out by enforced SSO (the owner break-glass and the operator's last resort), clock skew, a leaked SCIM token, and reading the `sso.*` / `scim.*` audit actions
- [tenant-export-and-deletion.md](tenant-export-and-deletion.md) — workspace export, offline verification and import; soft delete, operator restore and the crypto-shred purge (and what it leaves behind); the operator's part in access, rectification and erasure requests

Still to write: disk-full / bucket quota; migrating storage adapter; migrating between hosts. (Scaling worker concurrency is covered by queue-backlog.md, investor notification by incident-response.md.)
